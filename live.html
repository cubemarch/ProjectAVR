Notes

Anaglyph and Difference are alignment tools. In Difference a correctly-aligned rig shows horizontal edges cancelling to black; anything that leaves bright horizontal banding is vertical misalignment, which is the one stereo error that reliably causes discomfort.

Neither of those finds swapped eyes. Difference is symmetric — it shows the same picture whichever eye is which — and Anaglyph fuses happily into inverted depth. To check eye order, hold a finger 20–30 cm from the lenses against a distant vertical edge and compare the two halves in Side by side: the finger should sit further right of that edge in the left half than in the right half. If it sits further left, the eyes are swapped.

Swap left / right eye corrects that on this screen only. It is a diagnostic, not a fix: the HLS stream, the recording and every other viewer still carry the original order. Swapped eyes have to be corrected at the capture client, which owns the mapping from lens to eye.
